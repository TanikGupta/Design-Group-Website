Policies for Rape and Murder Cases in India

Policies for Rape Cases

  • Victim Compensation Scheme:
    Financial compensation for rape victims under the Nirbhaya Fund.
    Each state has its own compensation policy (minimum ₹3–5 lakh, can go higher).
  • Fast Track Special Courts (FTSCs):
    Special courts set up to handle rape and POCSO cases quickly.
    Target: Case disposal within 6 months.
  • Zero FIR Policy:
    Victim can file FIR at any police station (even if crime didn’t happen there).
    Police must register it and transfer it to the correct jurisdiction.
  • Witness Protection Scheme, 2018:
    Protection for rape victims and witnesses during investigation and trial.
    Measures include changing identity, relocation, security guards.
  • One-Stop Centers (OSC):
    Set up by the government to provide medical, legal, psychological, and police help in one place for rape victims.
  • Time-Bound Investigation:
    Rape investigations must be completed within 2 months (under CrPC amendments after 2018).

Policies for Murder Cases

  • Rarest of Rare Policy for Death Penalty:
    Courts use the "rarest of rare" principle to decide if death penalty should be given in murder cases.
  • Police Reforms (Supreme Court Guidelines, 2006):
    Guidelines to ensure fair investigation in serious cases like murder.
  • Witness Protection Scheme:
    Same as rape cases — also applies to murder case witnesses.
  • Faster FIR and Chargesheet Filing:
    Supreme Court orders insist on quick FIR registration and early filing of chargesheets to speed up murder trials.
