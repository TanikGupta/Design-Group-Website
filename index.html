Services: Support for survivors of sexual violence, manual scavenging, forced labour, and trafficking; legal aid; livelihood training.

Prerana

Services: Anti-human trafficking initiatives; protection and rehabilitation of women and children in red-light areas; child protection services.

Contacts: +91-22-2437 7446 / +91-22-2436 4505

preranaantitrafficking.org

SheSays India

Services: Online support for survivors of gender-based violence; legal and psychological resources; safe reporting guides.

shesays.in

Human Rights Law Network (HRLN)

Services: Free legal aid and litigation support for human rights violations; advocacy in areas like women’s rights, child rights, health, and disability law.

hrln.org

Safecity

Services: Anonymous reporting platform for sexual harassment in public spaces; data-driven advocacy and community safety programs.

safecity.in

Swayam (West Bengal)

Helpline: +91-33-2486 5559 / +91-98300 28317
