Rape and Murder Laws in India

Rape Laws (India)

  • Section 375 IPC: Defines rape (non-consensual sexual intercourse under specific conditions).
  • Section 376 IPC: Punishment for rape — minimum 10 years to life imprisonment + fine.
  • Section 376AB IPC: Rape of girl under 12 years — minimum 20 years jail, life imprisonment, or death penalty.
  • POCSO Act, 2012: Special law for sexual offences against children (under 18 years).
  • Criminal Law (Amendment) Act, 2013: Made laws stricter after Nirbhaya case (new definitions, higher punishments).
  • Criminal Law (Amendment) Act, 2018: Introduced death penalty for rape of girls below 12 years.
  • Fast Track Courts: Special courts to speed up rape trials.
  • Victim Identity Protection: Media and others are banned from disclosing rape victim’s identity (Section 228A IPC).

Murder Laws (India)

  • Section 302 IPC: Punishment for murder — death penalty or life imprisonment + fine.
  • Section 304 IPC: Punishment for culpable homicide not amounting to murder (lesser intent).
  • Rarest of Rare Doctrine: Death penalty only in rarest of rare cases (guidelines by Supreme Court).
  • Juvenile Justice Act, 2015: Different treatment for murderers below 18 years of age.
  • Immediate FIR Registration: Police must file FIR immediately in murder cases.
